# Users and jobs to be done

**Status:** Accepted for V0
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24
**Source:** [MVP specification](../sources.md)

## Companies and job posters

Organizations or individuals trying to fill an open role with trusted referrals.

They need to:

- submit a job with minimal maintenance;
- reach a relevant, trusted network;
- understand the quality and origin of referrals;
- avoid traditional recruiter-style upfront costs.

## Referrers

Community members who know potential candidates and are willing to forward relevant roles.

They need to:

- scan relevant jobs quickly;
- understand the expected effort and potential bonus;
- share a job in the channel they already use;
- trust that attribution and any later payout will be handled fairly.

## Candidates

People who receive an opportunity through somebody they trust.

They need to:

- understand the role and company quickly;
- know why they received the opportunity;
- avoid unnecessary disclosure of personal data;
- express interest or continue to an application with little friction.

## Internal operator

The team member who keeps the V0 marketplace usable.

They need to:

- review ingested jobs;
- correct parsing errors and fill missing bonus information;
- activate, archive, or manually add listings;
- investigate referral and ingestion events.

Supabase Studio is the initial operator interface. A dedicated admin product is optional for V0.
