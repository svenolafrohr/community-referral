# Success and validation

**Status:** Proposed measurement plan
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24
**Sources:** [MVP specification](../sources.md), [planning session](../sources.md)

## Demonstrable MVP

The MVP is demonstrable when:

- a live or shareable web app is available;
- active jobs can be viewed in a listing and detail flow;
- at least one realistic submission or import path works;
- at least one unique share or referral link works end to end;
- approximately five target users can receive and test it;
- feedback from real use can be captured;
- the business-model hypothesis is concrete enough to discuss with users.

## Primary behavioral signal

A target-community member shares a real opportunity with a suitable person in under one minute, and the team can determine whether the action led to interest.

## Learning questions

- Can users find a job worth sharing?
- Do they understand the referral bonus and conditions?
- Which sharing channel do they choose?
- Where do they hesitate or abandon the flow?
- Does the recipient understand the opportunity and trust the source?
- Are companies willing to provide a meaningful bonus or pay for distribution?

## Near-term validation plan

1. Seed five to ten credible jobs with complete bonus and source context.
2. Put the listing, detail, and sharing journey in front of approximately five target users.
3. Observe whether they can identify and share a relevant job without explanation.
4. Ask recipients about trust, clarity, privacy, and desired next action.
5. Ask job posters about bonus size and willingness to pay for outcomes or distribution.
6. Record findings in a dated file under `docs/product/research/` once the first study exists.
7. Convert only evidence-backed hypotheses into accepted decisions.

## Suggested early metrics

These definitions are proposed and need baselines:

- **Qualified share rate:** percentage of test users who share at least one job with a person they consider suitable.
- **Time to share:** time from opening the listings page to completing a share action.
- **Recipient engagement:** percentage of referral-link visits that reach the intended next action.
- **Listing quality:** percentage of active jobs that a target user considers clear, credible, and relevant enough to share.
- **Supply freshness:** percentage of active jobs confirmed as current within the chosen freshness window.
