# Vision and product principles

**Status:** Accepted for V0
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-24
**Sources:** [MVP specification](../sources.md), [planning session](../sources.md)

## Product in one sentence

A community-first referral tool that makes trusted job opportunities visible, easy to share, and attributable while keeping referral bonuses understandable.

## Opportunity

Hiring opportunities and recommendations already move through private communities, email threads, and personal messages. That distribution carries trust but lacks structure: jobs are easy to miss, referral terms are unclear, sharing is difficult to attribute, and companies have no lightweight way to activate a community.

The product turns that behavior into simple infrastructure:

- collect and structure job opportunities;
- make them discoverable inside a trusted community;
- make a relevant opportunity easy to share;
- explain the bonus and referral process;
- capture lightweight signals from sharing through expressed interest;
- leave room for later settlement without requiring payment infrastructure in V0.

## Working definition of V0

V0 succeeds when a person in the target community can find a real opportunity, share it with a suitable person in under one minute, and leave enough of a trail for the team to understand what was shared and whether it generated interest.

## Principles

### Community first, not a public marketplace

Start inside a known network. A bounded community reduces spam, improves relevance, and gives referrals credible social context.

### Trust over scale

A small number of good, current jobs is more valuable than a large supply of low-quality listings. Quality, provenance, and clarity should win over growth mechanics.

### Invisible infrastructure

The product enables existing relationships; it does not position itself as a recruiter or actively match candidates. It makes opportunities visible, sharing easy, and bonus logic transparent.

### Fast path to a real-world signal

V0 optimizes for a genuine share and a response from a real person. Features that do not improve that learning loop should normally wait.

### Privacy by default

Candidate data is not collected merely because the schema can hold it. Personal information requires a clear user action, purpose, and consent path.
